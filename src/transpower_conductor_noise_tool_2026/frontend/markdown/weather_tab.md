# Weather Tab

_Placeholder - content to be expanded._

The Weather tab shows a wind rose (a compass-style chart with 16 direction
sectors, where each sector's length shows how often the wind blew from that
direction and its colour shows the average wind speed when it did) and a
monthly rainfall chart for a chosen site. An optional start/end month+year
range scopes both charts, and also enables an additional chart of monthly
minimum/maximum/mean rainfall and wind speed over that range. With no range
selected, both charts show the site's full history.
